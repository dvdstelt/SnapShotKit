This agreement is between you and Dennis van der Stelt, who owns the copyright in SnapShotKit. It covers every contribution you make to the project, now and in future.

It exists for one reason. SnapShotKit is source-available rather than open source, and it may one day be sold under other terms as well. If each contributor kept an unlicensed hold over their own patch, none of that would be possible without finding every person who ever touched the file. Asking once, before the first merge, is the honest version of that problem.

Please read it. It is short on purpose.

## Signing

Post this exact sentence as a comment on your pull request:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

A bot records your GitHub username and the time, in [`signatures/version1/cla.json`](signatures/version1/cla.json) on the `cla-signatures` branch of this repository. You sign once, and every pull request you open afterwards is covered.

If you would rather not sign, that is a reasonable position and no hard feelings. Open an issue describing the change instead, and you will be credited in the commit that implements it.
